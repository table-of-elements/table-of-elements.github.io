Eisenhower-Matrix

The Eisenhower Matrix is a prioritization tool that categorizes tasks based on their urgency and importance. It helps you focus on the most important tasks and delegate or eliminate those that are less important or less urgent.

Effort: small Difficulty: low Effectiveness: short

The Eisenhower matrix can be used individually or in a team to prioritize tasks and support daily task management.

💡 Tip from practice: Use the Eisenhower Matrix regularly to keep your task list current.
