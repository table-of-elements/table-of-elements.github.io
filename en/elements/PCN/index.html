Project Canvas

The project canvas is a visual tool for depicting the most important aspects of a project on one page. It helps to clearly structure the project and present important elements in a clear way. It also helps to achieve a common understanding.

Effort: small Difficulty: medium Effectiveness: short

Like any canvas, the Project Canvas really comes into its own when used in a team. Especially at the beginning of a project, it helps to gain a common understanding of the most important key points and framework conditions of the project.

💡 Tip from practice: Fill in the individual fields in silence at the beginning. This helps to give everyone an equal say and to slow down the extroverts a little.
